Frequently Asked Questions

What is an "asset"?

An asset is any resource you schedule shifts for — a lab, room, piece of equipment, workstation, or time block. The tier limits how many assets you can create in a project.

Is this a subscription?

Yes. ShiftGrid uses annual licenses. Your subscription includes all updates and new features released during the year. An active license is required to use the software.

Can I upgrade my tier later?

Yes. Contact us to upgrade from Starter to Professional or Enterprise. We'll credit your existing license toward the upgrade cost.

What happens when my trial or license expires?

An active license is required to use ShiftGrid. When your trial or license expires, you'll need to purchase or renew to continue using the software. Your project files are stored locally and are never deleted.

Is there a limit on teams or team members?

No. All tiers support unlimited teams, roster members, and shift demands. Only the number of assets is limited by tier.
