Features

ShiftGrid is built for teams that share limited resources and need schedules that respect real-world constraints.

Visual Schedule Grid

See your entire schedule at a glance. The grid displays assets as rows and days as columns, with each cell showing available shift slots and their utilization.

  • Color-coded shifts by team
  • Utilization percentages per day
  • Clear visibility of open slots
  • Configurable date range
[Schedule Grid Screenshot]

Drag-and-Drop Scheduling

Drag shifts from the backlog to the schedule, or move them between days and assets. Incompatible placements are automatically blocked.

  • Drag from backlog to schedule
  • Move shifts between days
  • Incompatible assets are greyed out
  • Instant visual feedback
[Drag-and-Drop Screenshot]

Auto-Scheduler

One click to schedule all backlog shifts while respecting every constraint. Choose between two scheduling modes based on your needs.

  • Spread Mode — distributes shifts evenly across the schedule period
  • Compress Mode — packs shifts as densely as possible
  • Respects all constraints automatically
  • Configurable consecutive day limits
[Auto-Scheduler Screenshot]

Auto-Scheduler Diagnostics

When shifts can't be placed, ShiftGrid tells you exactly why. The diagnostics modal breaks down failures by reason and by team.

  • Summary of placed vs. failed shifts
  • Bar chart of rejection reasons
  • Per-team breakdown with details
  • Review anytime via Results button
[Diagnostics Modal Screenshot]

Team Member Availability

Track when each team member is unavailable. Import PTO and vacation dates from spreadsheets. The scheduler respects availability automatically.

  • Per-person unavailable date tracking
  • CSV import with flexible date formats
  • Merge or replace existing dates
  • Warnings when scheduling conflicts
[Availability Calendar Screenshot]

Flexible Demand Patterns

Define shift demands as fixed quantities or recurring patterns. Recurring demands automatically adjust when your schedule dates change.

  • Fixed Quantity — exact number of shifts
  • Every Weekday — one shift per weekday
  • N per Week — specific count per week
  • Specific Day — every Monday, first Tuesday, etc.
[Demand Modal Screenshot]

Asset Capabilities

Tag assets with capabilities like "High Security" or "Equipment X". Demands can require specific capabilities, and shifts will only be placed in compatible assets.

  • Custom capability tags per asset
  • Required capabilities on demands
  • Automatic compatibility filtering
  • Visual indicators during drag-and-drop
[Capabilities Screenshot]

CSV Import & Export

Import existing schedules and team availability from spreadsheets. Export final schedules for sharing or integration with other systems.

  • Import schedules with conflict resolution
  • Import team member availability
  • Export schedules with all details
  • Flexible date and name format detection
[Import Wizard Screenshot]

Built-In Constraints

ShiftGrid enforces rules that spreadsheets can't. These constraints are checked during drag-and-drop and enforced by the auto-scheduler.

Consecutive Day Limits Configurable 1-14 day maximum
Late-to-Early Protection No early shift after a late shift
Max Shifts Per Day Limit shifts per team per day (1-5)
POC Availability Minimum team members must be available
Asset Compatibility Match capabilities to requirements
Days of Operation Respect asset operating schedules
