Turn Constraints Into Schedules

ShiftGrid is a scheduling engine designed for teams that share limited resources — computers, equipment, rooms, or time blocks — and need schedules that actually respect real-world constraints.

Resource-Aware Scheduling

Define assets, operating hours, shift lengths, and capabilities so work is assigned where it can actually happen — without conflicts or overbooking.

Constraint-Driven Logic

Automatically respects rules like consecutive work limits, required rest periods, availability windows, and sequencing constraints that spreadsheets can’t enforce.

How It Works

  1. Define resources and shift structures
  2. Specify demands and coverage requirements
  3. Apply constraints and rules
  4. Generate a conflict-aware schedule
